Heat
Growth: a region's share of the arrivals in the heat window over its share of the base map. Volume: the arrivals themselves. Brightest is highest.
Timeline
Posts per bin; orange is the share stranded at placement; green ticks are rebuilds. Click ahead to run to a time, drag back to rewind.
Lineage
One band per region identity, height = living posts; forks are splits and merges at rebuilds. Hover to magnify, click a band to focus it.
Method
Every item is turned into a vector of numbers that captures its meaning (an embedding; for text, MiniLM with 384 numbers), so similar items get similar vectors. The starting map is a UMAP layout of those vectors, fitted on this GPU: similar items sit together. Each new item is then placed beside its nearest neighbours on the map, and the map is quietly re-laid out every so often so that it keeps fitting what has arrived (the project's names for these steps: Batched RevAdopt and NodeGardener). Chips name a region by its most distinctive words; +N counts what arrived since Play. Click a chip for the region, a dot for the item.
